How to label (tap to open)
  • Drag a tight box around every dog you can see, puppies included. Include the visible part of a partly hidden dog.
  • Mark a box Hard when the dog is mostly hidden (under about a quarter visible) or too small or blurry to be sure. Hard boxes are not counted for or against the model.
  • No dog in the frame? Tap No dogs. Can't tell at all? Tap Skip frame.
  • Every box is listed under the image. Tap ✕ next to a box to delete it, or tap Box N to select it and then Hard. To fix a box, delete it and draw again.
  • Small dogs far away: tap Zoom, switch to Pan to move around, then back to Draw.
  • Your work saves automatically. You can stop and come back any time.
